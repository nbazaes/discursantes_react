# SeleccionarDomingo — El Árbol de Generaciones

Scope and mode: the Sunday-planning route, the product's core surface. Mode:
Operate. Mobile-first (the real scene is a leader planning on a phone between
meetings).

Audience: ward leaders and secretaries organizing sacrament-meeting speakers;
Spanish-first (Chile), English as full translation.

Job / task: pick a Sunday date, see who is most due (fair rotation), assign
speakers and topics, save, and notify via WhatsApp — all in minutes, with a
trustworthy record.

Proof / content: the exact rotation counter ("hace 214 días") on every
suggestion; the never-spoken amber mark; the generation of assigned speakers on
the ruled line; WhatsApp notify as the second action.

Constraints: ward data isolation (RLS), ES/EN + dark mode, Spanish-first LDS
voice, WhatsApp flow, and the pinned purple/amber palette.

Chosen direction: El Árbol de Generaciones — the Sunday as a generation of
connected nodes. Pending suggestions wait on dashed connectors; assigned
speakers bead onto the line. Memorable moment: tapping a suggestion "wires" the
speaker into the generation with a flash.

Unresolved decisions: none at the world level; authenticated render is pending
the user's signed-in visual confirmation.
